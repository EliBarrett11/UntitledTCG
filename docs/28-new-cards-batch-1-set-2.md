# New Cards Batch 1, set 2 of 4: approved by Elliott (23 November 2026)

All eight cards, the four new rules (On Attack, On Dawn, Undying, Soaring) and the three Prime revisions are approved. The art prompts are in `docs/29-art-prompts-batch1-set2.md`.

Same curve as set 1 (rulebook 7.1):
- Attack + Health = **Level + 2, less 1 per ability**.
- Shield, Ward and "Prime:" lines are free.
- Prime adds +1/+1.

## New rules this set needs (each for approval)
- **On Attack** (trigger): resolves when the creature attacks, after the target is chosen and before damage. (1 ability.)
- **On Dawn** (trigger): resolves at each Dawn while the creature is on the battlefield, at the Dawn healing step. (1 ability.)
- **Undying** (keyword): the first time it would be defeated, it's left at 1 Health instead. Once per creature. (1 ability.)
- **Soaring** (keyword): Shields can't guard against its attacks. Wards still can, if it's Arcane. (1 ability.)

## 5. Hunter's Snare (Action, Ranger)
- **React (1):** the attacker takes 2 damage.
- **Adapt (2):** an enemy creature becomes Spent and gets −2 Attack until Dusk.
- **Strat (3):** each enemy creature in a Location becomes Spent.

## 6. Second Wind (Action, Fighter)
- **React (1):** heal 3 to your creature being attacked.
- **Adapt (2):** one of your Spent creatures becomes Active.
- **Strat (3):** each of your Spent creatures in a Location becomes Active and gets +1 Attack until Dusk.

## Revisions: the three Prime effects that fell flat
Elliott said Prime On Summon effects that don't repay the wait feel flat. Proposed:

| Card | Prime now | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| Tilda, the Cinderwick Prodigy (Prime 1) | **On Summon:** 2 damage to an enemy creature. Often no target in Round 1. | **Prime: Ambush. On Summon:** 2 damage to an enemy creature **or the enemy Warlord**. |
| Elowen, the Duskmere Sibyl (Prime 6) | **On Summon:** draw 2 cards. | **Prime: On Summon:** draw 3 cards and gain 1 Energy. |
| Mossback Ancient (Prime 8) | **On Summon:** 1 damage to each enemy creature in its Location. | **Prime: On Summon:** 2 damage to each enemy creature in its Location, and heal 2 to each of your creatures there. |

**Art needed:** 16 images: four series at 3 each, plus Hunter's Snare, Second Wind, Venomglass Dagger and Gallows Hill.
