# Art prompts v2: the new style (batch 1)

**For Elliott to generate with ChatGPT.** Written 17 November 2026 by the design chat, following `docs/17-art-style-guide.md`. Each image is 1536 × 1024 (3:2). Send them back named by id (or in this order) and the design chat will crop and commit them.

**Batch 1: the anchors.** One defining image for each Character and Monster series that doesn't have one yet, plus the two Legendaries and Lady Corwen. Batch 2 (the young and elder forms) attaches these as references, so the series age recognisably. Already done in the style tests: Gloomfang Wolf, Oathsworn Captain and Ironroot Pass.

## 1. Hollowfoot Knifemaster → `hollowfoot-knifemaster`

*Character · Halfling Rogue (Ambush)*

```
Create an original fantasy illustration for a collectible card game: a wide, cinematic full-art scene in landscape format, 3:2 (1536 × 1024).

Subject: the Hollowfoot Knifemaster, a halfling rogue in his thirties and a shameless show-off: quick, wiry, curly chestnut hair under a deep green hood pushed back, a sharp foxlike grin and one eyebrow raised as if he's winking at the viewer. Fitted dark leathers, a bandolier of gleaming throwing knives across his chest, a stolen pie balanced on one hand. He is caught mid-leap between two rooftops, three knives spinning in the air beside him as if he's juggling them for fun.

Setting: a crowded hillside city at night during a lantern festival. Paper lanterns strung between steep roofs, chimneys and laundry lines, a full moon behind clouds. Far below in the lit street, a red-faced baker and a city guard shout up at him, the guard's helmet plume neatly cut off. Fireworks bloom softly over the distant harbour.

Style: elegant, high-end digital painting with the polish of premium fantasy card-game splash art. Stylised but grounded: confident shapes, clear silhouettes, expressive, characterful faces, beautifully rendered materials, light and texture. A night palette of deep indigo and moonlit blue, warmed by glowing lantern orange and gold, with his green hood as a bright accent. Charismatic and full of story; heroic fantasy with warmth and wonder, not grim or gloomy. Painterly brushwork in the details, crisp focus on the subject, softer depth of field behind. Not photorealistic, not anime, not cartoonish, not 3D-rendered plastic.

Composition: the halfling and his spinning knives in the centre of the frame, his head in its upper half with clear space above, so a tall portrait crop from the centre (about half the width) frames him perfectly. Keep the lower third directly beneath simpler and slightly darker (dark slate rooftops and shadowed tiles), as card text will sit there. Use the full width for the scene and the story on either side.

Do not include any text, letters, logos, borders, frames, card layouts or watermarks.
```
