# New Cards Batch 1, set 4 of 4: approved by Elliott (23 November 2026)

All eight cards and the new rules (On Dusk, Tinker, Chill, Frenzy) and affiliations are approved. The art prompts are in `docs/33-art-prompts-batch1-set4.md`.

The last set. Same curve as before:
- Attack + Health = **Level + 2, less 1 per ability**.
- Shield, Ward and "Prime:" lines are free.
- Prime adds +1/+1.
- A Legendary sits above the curve, like The Ashen Wyrm (6/6 with one ability at Level 8).

## New rules this set needs (each for approval)
- **On Dusk** (trigger): resolves at each Dusk while the creature is on the battlefield, before dominance is scored. (1 ability.)
- **Tinker** (keyword): whenever you buy an Item from the Market, this gets +1/+1 for good. (1 ability.)
- **Chill** (keyword): a creature that damages this one becomes Spent. (1 ability.)
- **Frenzy** (keyword): while damaged, it has +2 Attack. (1 ability.)
- **New affiliations:**
  - the **Artificer** class;
  - the **Elemental** Monster kind;
  - two home groups: **Redmaw** (orc shamans) and **Cogwright** (a gnomish workshop guild).

## 5. Chain Lightning (Action, Wizard)
- **React (1):** 1 damage to the attacker and 1 damage to another enemy creature.
- **Adapt (2):** 2 damage to an enemy creature and 1 damage to each other enemy creature in its Location.
- **Strat (3):** 2 damage to each enemy creature in a Location.

## 6. Market Day (Event)
- **Release 1.** Gain 3 Gold.
- **Favour (a Gnome):** gain 5 Gold instead.

## 7. Night Raid (Event)
- **Release 1.** An enemy creature takes 2 damage and becomes Spent.
- **Favour (a Rogue):** each enemy creature in its Location becomes Spent too.

## 8. The Deepmaw (Legendary) and The Drowned Reach (its Location)
- **The Deepmaw:** Legendary Monster, **Beast**, **Slow**, Martial, Level 8, **4/8**, 3 lanes wide.
  - **On Summon:** each enemy creature in its Location becomes Spent.
- **The Drowned Reach** (3 lanes; it arrives and leaves with the Deepmaw):
  - **At Dusk,** each creature here takes 1 damage. The Deepmaw itself isn't affected.

**Art needed:** 17 images:
- four series at 3 each (12);
- Chain Lightning, Market Day and Night Raid;
- The Deepmaw;
- The Drowned Reach.

With this set the pool reaches 61 deck cards (29 + 32 new), plus the two Legendary Locations.
