# New Cards Batch 1, set 3 of 4: Campaigns, Items and Locations (proposals, 23 November 2026)

Campaign Creatures follow the same curve as creatures (Level + 2, less 1 per ability, +1/+1 at Prime; they always arrive at Prime). The size of the reward scales with the Quest's length: a Side-Quest gives a Level 4 creature, a mid-length Campaign Level 6, a long one Level 8. Every Quest uses a count the engine already tracks: defeats, dominations, damage to the enemy Warlord and Items bought. So this set needs **no new rules**.

## 1. The Smuggler's Run (Campaign: Side-Quest)
- **Quest:** buy 2 Items from the Market. *(About Rounds 2–3.)*
- **Order (1 Energy):** gain 1 Gold.
- **Campaign Creature: Wren, the Smuggler.** Halfling Rogue, Swift, Martial, Level 4, Prime, **4/3**.
  - **On Attack:** gain 1 Gold.

## 2. The Siege of Greywater (Campaign: mid-length)
- **Quest:** deal 6 damage to the enemy Warlord. *(About Rounds 4–5.)*
- **Order (2 Energy):** your creatures get +1 Attack until Dusk.
- **Campaign Creature: Brannoc, the Siegebreaker.** Orc Fighter, Steady, Martial, Level 6, Prime, **5/4**.
  - **On Attack:** if it attacks the enemy Warlord, it deals +2 damage.

## 3. The Grove Accord (Campaign: mid-length)
- **Quest:** dominate a Location at Dusk 3 times. *(About Rounds 4–6.)*
- **Order (2 Energy):** heal 3 to each of your creatures in a Location.
- **Campaign Creature: Oakheart, the Grove Guardian.** Treant (a Monster), Slow, Martial, **Shield**, Level 6, Prime, **3/6**.
  - **On Dawn:** heal 2 to each of your creatures in its Location.

## 4. The Ember Crown (Campaign: long)
- **Quest:** defeat 8 enemy creatures. *(Late game, the size of Lady Corwen's Commission.)*
- **Order (2 Energy):** 2 damage to an enemy creature.
- **Campaign Creature: Ysolde, the Ember Queen.** Human Wizard, Swift, Arcane, **Ward**, Level 8, Prime, **6/4**.
  - **Armour 1.**
  - **On Attack:** 2 damage to each other enemy creature in its target's Location.

## 5. Warhorn of the Vanguard (Item: Consumed, price 2)
- Your creatures in a Location get **+2 Attack** until Dusk.

## 6. Cinderfeather Amulet (Item: Held, price 3)
- **+1 Health.** Its holder has **Undying**.

## 7. The Sunlit Shrine (Location: 3 lanes, 3 Rounds)
- **At Dawn,** heal 2 to each creature here (both sides).

## 8. Stormbreak Crags (Location: 4 lanes, 2 Rounds)
- Creatures here have **+1 Attack and −1 Health** (never below 1 Health).

**Art needed:** 12 images: 4 Campaign cards, 4 Campaign Creatures, 2 Items and 2 Locations.

**Set 4 (the last)** will have 2 Character series, 2 Monster series, 1 Action, 2 Events and a second Legendary with its own Location. That brings the pool to about 63 cards.
