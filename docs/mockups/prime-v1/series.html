PRIME SUMMONS · ONE SERIES, SIX ROUNDS

Oathsworn Recruit series · Mid · Prime in Round 4 · approved stats. Every copy of a series is at the same Level each Round, so its Prime is the same Round for everyone.

THE SERIES CURVE · HOVER PREVIEW AND CODEX

Shown when you hover a creature in hand or the Market, and under each series in the Codex. The bar is value against the curve for that Level: full on the curve, overflowing gold at Prime.

READING IT

Rising (silver tag) shows the Round its Prime arrives: hold it with Retain, or summon it early for tempo.

Prime (gold tag, foil frame, gold glow in hand) is the series at its best: +1 Attack and +1 Health above the curve, +1 Renown when summoned, and any “Prime:” ability works.

Waning (muted tag) is past its best but still worth its Energy: on the curve, with its elder form’s ability.

From Round 6 every card sits at its last Level for the rest of the match, so a series with its Prime last is a late-game threat, and one that peaks early is an opener.
