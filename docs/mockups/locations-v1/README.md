# Location tiles, mockup v1 (design chat, 16 November 2026)

Elliott's feedback: Locations should look like tiles laid on the overworld map, bold foreground pieces rather than a transparent tint, and the Location's info in the middle should look much more polished.

- `before.png`: the game at V3 (`?seed=19&scene=mid`).
- `after.png`, `after-crop.png`: the same moment with `mock.css` and the plaque injected by `shot2.mjs`.

**The tile:** the Location's art at full strength, clipped to a rounded tile with a dark edge, a brass hairline and an outer rim in the Location's colour, a darker base edge so it sits raised on the map, and a drop shadow. A soft vignette at the top and bottom keeps the cards readable. Empty spaces on a tile are dark glass with a dashed brass outline.

**The label (v2, after Elliott's notes):** no box and no emblem. The name sits straight on the painting in gold Cinzel, over a soft dark glow that fades out at every edge so the art shows around it. Under it: a fine brass rule with a small diamond, the effect with its number in gold ("**+1 Attack** to creatures here"), and a line of diamonds for the Rounds left, then "2 ROUNDS LEFT · YOURS", with "YOURS" in your colour (crimson) or "ENEMY'S" in blue.

**Approved by Elliott, 16 November 2026, with those changes.**

The effect wording and the emblem come from the card data in the build; the mockup typed them.
