*Attach the style reference `assets/cards-v3/gloomfang-wolf.jpg`.*

```
Create an original fantasy illustration for a collectible card game: a cinematic full-art frame in landscape format, 3:2 (1536 × 1024), like a still from a painterly animated fantasy film. Match the painting style, brushwork and lighting of the attached style reference, but not its subject.

Subject: The Smuggler's Run: a small black-sailed boat moored in a hidden sea cave, crates and barrels stacked on a rickety wooden jetty, a single hooded lantern glowing on a post, a rope ladder leading up into darkness, coins spilling from a split sack.

Style: a frame from a high-end, painterly animated fantasy film. Stylised 3D-feeling forms with hand-painted textures and visible brushstrokes on skin, hair, fur, cloth and metal; slightly stylised proportions with bold, graphic shape design and a strong, readable silhouette. Backgrounds painted loosely like cinematic concept art, softer than the subject. Rich, vibrant colour scripting: deep sea-green and black water against a warm amber lantern and silver moonlight, with saturated complementary accents and bold rim lighting from one motivated key light; atmospheric depth and a shallow-focus lens feel. Expressive, intense acting through the eyes, brow and posture, but restrained: no wide grins or comic faces. Serious, beautiful and cinematic. Not photorealistic, not cel-shaded cartoon, not anime, not glossy plastic 3D.

Composition: the boat and the lantern-lit jetty is the focal landmark, in the centre of the frame, so a tall portrait crop from the centre (about half the width) frames it perfectly; it must also read as a wide panorama. Keep the richest detail around the landmark and calmer edges. Keep the lower third simpler and darker (dark water in shadow), as text will sit there. No figures.
```
