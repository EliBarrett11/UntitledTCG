# Card design principles (Elliott, 23 November 2026)

The standards every new card is designed against. This is built up from the card design discussion; it governs New Cards Batch 1 (about 30 new cards, bringing the pool to about 60).

## Goals
1. **Cards are effective and fun to play in their own right.** Each card should be worth playing on its own merits, not only as a piece of a combo.
2. **No designed decks and no ready-made decks.** Cards aren't designed with a particular deck or style in mind. Players discover deck styles themselves, as they emerge naturally from the growing card pool.
3. **Loose synergy across the pool.** Most cards work with any other card. Some cards synergise specifically with card groups, such as Fighters, Monsters, Elves or any other affiliation, without locking a deck into that group.
4. **No balancing until all 60 cards are in.** The full pool is play-tested raw first; balance tools come after that.

5. **What makes a card fun** (Elliott's words, summarised):
   - its stats, keywords, triggers, effects and affiliations are combined in a unique, interesting and exciting way;
   - it is useful in any Round;
   - it rewards being played at the right moment;
   - it synergises with other cards and makes the deck better;
   - it gives a good return for the resources spent on it.
   - **Fun examples:** the Spire Magus defeating an enemy on summon; the Deepforge healer's On Summon heal; Actions whose React turns the opponent's turn at the perfect moment, and whose Strat makes the opponent predict and counter.
   - **Fell flat:** Prime On Summon effects that aren't strong enough to repay saving the card for its Prime.
6. **No limit on what one card does,** as long as it pays for extra things with its core stats. Explore multiple keywords, triggers and effects, and modes the player chooses between. Be very creative, and make the most of the game's features.
7. **All affiliations are fair game:** ancestries, classes, Monster kinds and home groups. They must be clear on the card and in the Card Library, so players can see what synergises.
8. **New mechanics are welcome.** Elliott approves every new card.
9. **Campaigns.**
   - **More Campaign cards are needed.** A deck may hold up to 3 Campaigns.
   - **Three lengths:**
     - Side-Quests, meant to be completed in the early game;
     - mid-length Campaigns, for the mid-game;
     - long Campaigns like Lady Corwen's Commission, for the late game.
   - **Campaign Creatures** (like Aldric) are not Legendaries.
