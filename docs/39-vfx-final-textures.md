## The ten prompts

### 1. `vfx-vines`

*Used for:* Nature: Hunter's Snare, the Thornwood (Ilyra), Treants (Mossback, Oakheart), The Grove Accord, Rally binding.

```
Create an original visual-effects texture for a fantasy card game, in the painting style of the attached style reference: hand-painted with visible brushstrokes, bold graphic shapes, crisp designed edges, stylised like effects in a painterly animated fantasy film.

Square 1024 × 1024. A grid of 3 × 3 separate curling vines and thorny tendrils, each a bold painted shape of deep green stems with a few bright leaves and sharp thorns, some coiled, some lashing straight, some in a loop like a snare, every vine clearly separated with black space. On a pure black background (#000000), nothing else in the frame: the black will be removed and the objects kept solid.

No text, letters, symbols that read as letters, borders or watermarks.
```
