## 1. Cinderwick Prodigy → `assets/cards/cinderwick-prodigy.jpg`

```
Create an original fantasy illustration for a collectible card game.

Subject: A gnome child of about ten, small and wiry, wild copper hair singed at the tips, oversized scholar's robes rolled at the sleeves, holding a cupped ball of living flame in both hands with fierce delight. Freckles, soot on the cheeks, eyes reflecting fire. Wizard.

Setting: A cluttered tower study at night, scorched books and melted candles around her.

Composition: vertical 5:7 portrait. Place the subject in the upper-middle of the frame, with the head about one third of the way down. Keep the bottom 40% of the image darker and simpler, because card text will sit over it. Leave some breathing room at the top.

Do not include any text, letters, logos, borders, frames, card layouts or watermarks.
```
