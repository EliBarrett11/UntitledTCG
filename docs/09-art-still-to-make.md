# Art still to make

**What this is:** every image still outstanding, with its ready-to-paste prompt, so nothing is forgotten. Written 14 November 2026, when 36 of 37 card illustrations and 94 of 113 VFX textures were in.

**How to deliver:** send the images to the design chat in any order. It identifies, processes and files them (card art in `assets/cards/`, effects in `assets/vfx/`), and updates this list. Until an image arrives, the game draws a stand-in, so nothing is blocked.

## 1. Card illustration

### 1. Rumour of Treasure → `assets/cards/rumour-of-treasure.jpg`

```
Create an original fantasy illustration for a collectible card game.

Subject: A hooded stranger leaning across a tavern table, sliding an old map with a marked cross to two eager adventurers.

Setting: A smoky tavern corner, lit by a single candle with a plum-glass shade.

Style: mature, epic, painterly dark high fantasy, like a richly rendered oil painting crossed with high-end card-game splash art. Visible but refined brushwork, realistic anatomy and textures, cinematic lighting, deep shadows. A muted, earthy palette with deep plum as the only strong accent. Grounded and atmospheric. Not anime, not cartoon, not chibi, not 3D-rendered, not glossy.

Composition: vertical 5:7 portrait. Place the subject in the upper-middle of the frame, with the head about one third of the way down. Keep the bottom 40% of the image darker and simpler, because card text will sit over it. Leave some breathing room at the top.

Do not include any text, letters, logos, borders, frames, card layouts or watermarks.
```
