# New Cards Batch 1, set 1 of 4: approved by Elliott (23 November 2026)

All eight cards and the four new rules (Rally, Venom, Choose one, "Prime: Choose both") are approved. The art prompts are in `docs/27-art-prompts-batch1-set1.md`.

Designed to `docs/23-card-design-principles.md` and the stat curve (rulebook 7.1):
- A Rising or Waning creature's Attack + Health = **Level + 2, less 1 per ability**.
- Shield, Ward and "Prime:" abilities are free.
- **At Prime, a creature gets +1 Attack and +1 Health.**
- **A creature with a choice of modes** pays for its strongest mode plus 1.


## New rules this set needs (each for approval)
- **Rally (group):** resolves when another creature of that group is summoned on your side. *Rally (Fighter): …* (1 ability.)
- **Venom:** after this creature deals damage to a creature, that creature becomes Spent. (1 ability.)
- **Choose one:** an On Summon with modes. The player picks the mode as part of the Summon action. (Costs its strongest mode + 1.)
- **"Prime: Choose both."** At Prime, a choose-one card does every mode. (Free, like all Prime lines.)

## 4. Shield Wall (Action, Fighter)
- **React (1):** your creature being attacked gets **Armour 2** until Dusk.
- **Adapt (2):** your creatures in a Location get **+2 Health** until Dusk.
- **Strat (3):** your creatures in a Location get **+1 Attack and +2 Health** for good.

## 5. Glass Veil (Action, Seer)
- **React (1):** return your creature being attacked to your hand. The attack does nothing.
- **Adapt (2):** return an enemy creature of Level 4 or less to its owner's hand.
- **Strat (3):** return an enemy creature to its owner's hand.

*Why it's fun:* a rescue at the perfect moment, and a Strat the opponent has to guess at.

## 6. War Drums (Event)
- **Release 1.** Your creatures get **+1 Attack** until Dusk.
- **Favour (an Orc):** your creatures get **+2 Attack** until Dusk instead.

## 7. The Bounty Board (Campaign: Side-Quest)
- **Quest:** defeat 3 enemy creatures.
- **Order (1 Energy):** gain 1 Gold.
- **Campaign Creature: Rook, the Bounty Hunter:** Human Rogue, Swift, Martial, Level 4, Prime, **4/3, Ambush**.
- Reachable by about Round 3. A small, fast reward that arrives ready to attack.

## 8. The Ashfall (The Ashen Wyrm's Location)
- **3 lanes.** It arrives with the Wyrm and leaves when the Wyrm is defeated.
- **Creatures here have −1 Attack** (never below 0). The Wyrm itself isn't affected.
- Stage R builds it with this text until it's approved.

**Art needed for this set:** 15 images (three series at 3 each, plus Shield Wall, Glass Veil, War Drums, The Bounty Board, Rook and The Ashfall).
