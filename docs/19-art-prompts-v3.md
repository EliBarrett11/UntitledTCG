# Art prompts v3: the final style (18 November 2026)

**Locked style (Elliott, 18 November 2026):** painterly cinematic animation, restrained but expressive acting, one subject, with more colour than the three tests. Style references: `assets/cards-v3/deepforge-priest.jpg`, `oathsworn-captain.jpg` and `gloomfang-wolf.jpg` (already final).

## How to keep every image in this style

1. **Attach one style reference to every prompt.** Use the reference closest in subject: Garrick for humans and knights, Brenna for dwarves, magic and interiors, the wolf for creatures and landscapes. The prompt tells the generator to copy its painting style, not its subject.
2. **For a young or elder form, attach its series' image as well,** so the character stays the same person.
3. **Never add crowds, onlookers or comic props.** If an image comes back with them, regenerate it.
4. **If it looks too realistic,** add "more stylised, more visible painted brushwork" to the end of the Style paragraph. **If it looks too dull,** add "more vivid colour".

## The reusable template

```
Create an original fantasy illustration for a collectible card game: a cinematic full-art frame in landscape format, 3:2 (1536 × 1024), like a still from a painterly animated fantasy film. Match the painting style, brushwork and lighting of the attached style reference, but not its subject.

Subject: …

Setting: …

Style: a frame from a high-end, painterly animated fantasy film. Stylised 3D-feeling forms with hand-painted textures and visible brushstrokes on skin, hair, fur, cloth and metal; slightly stylised proportions with bold, graphic shape design and a strong, readable silhouette. Backgrounds painted loosely like cinematic concept art, softer than the subject. Rich, vibrant colour scripting: <the card's palette>, with saturated complementary accents and bold rim lighting from one motivated key light; atmospheric depth and a shallow-focus lens feel. Expressive, intense acting through the eyes, brow and posture, but restrained: no wide grins or comic faces. Serious, beautiful and cinematic. Not photorealistic, not cel-shaded cartoon, not anime, not glossy plastic 3D.

Composition: <subject> in the centre of the frame, its head in its upper half with clear space above, so a tall portrait crop from the centre (about half the width) frames it perfectly. Keep the lower third directly beneath simpler and darker (<simple ground>), as card text will sit there. Use the full width for atmosphere, with soft, uncluttered edges. No other figures.

Any glyphs or runes are abstract shapes, never letters. Do not include any text, letters, logos, borders, frames, card layouts or watermarks.
```
